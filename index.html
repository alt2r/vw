The Band

VAIN WILD. Every decade a British Rock n' Roll band comes around to set fire to the musical landscape. VAIN WILD aims to be that band. With a sound heavily inspired by the 80s Glam Metal movement, they have grown massively on social media for their refreshing take on the genre and their magnetic presence. Having toured the country in support of their 2026 debut album, not a single person leaves a VAIN WILD show unsatisfied. VAIN WILD is Artem, James Brown and Harry Hallifax
